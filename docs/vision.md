# Product Vision — PMAI: AI Assistant for Product Managers

---

## Vision Statement

> Empower every Product Manager to make faster, smarter, and more customer-centric decisions — by turning raw feedback into strategy, and strategy into execution-ready sprints, in minutes rather than days.

---

## North Star

**PMAI becomes the PM's second brain** — a trusted co-pilot that remembers everything, surfaces what matters, and drafts what's needed so PMs can spend their energy on judgment, not logistics.

---

## The World We Want to Create

Today, great product decisions depend on the individual PM's ability to synthesize chaos — hundreds of feedback signals, competing stakeholder opinions, incomplete data, and tight deadlines. This reliance on heroic individual effort means quality is inconsistent and PMs burn out.

In the world PMAI creates:
- Customer feedback is automatically ingested, clustered, and ranked by business impact.
- Every PM has a clear, evidence-backed view of what customers want most.
- Roadmaps are grounded in customer evidence, not just stakeholder pressure.
- Sprint planning is prepared and organized before the meeting starts.
- A stakeholder-ready sprint summary is generated in seconds, not 30 minutes.
- Product knowledge is retained, searchable, and grows smarter over time.

---

## The Core Flow

PMAI is organised around a single end-to-end workflow that takes a PM from raw customer signal to shipped feature:

```
Customer Feedback
      ↓
Feedback Hub — keyword-based theme detection with verbatim evidence (zero hallucination)
      ↓
Pipeline Approval — PM reviews themes + evidence before any artefact is created
      ↓
Documents — AI-generated PRD, User Stories, AC; inline editing with full version history
      ↓
Insights — AI answers "what do customers want most?"
      ↓
Roadmap — themes become prioritised roadmap items
      ↓
Sprint Planner — items become sprint-ready user stories
      ↓
Sprint Brief — AI generates stakeholder summary
      ↓
Feature Ships — traceable from pain to delivered value
```

Each step is connected. A customer complaint on Monday becomes a shipped feature on Friday, with every step assisted by AI. The PM stays in control of the decisions; PMAI removes the busywork between them.

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Understand — Turn Feedback into Insight
Aggregate customer feedback from any source and surface themes using deterministic keyword matching. Every theme is accompanied by a verbatim evidence excerpt from the actual feedback — no hallucination, no invented patterns. The PM sees exactly which sentence triggered each theme before approving any artefact.

### 2. Document — AI-Generated Specs with Human Control
From approved themes, PMAI generates PRDs, User Stories, and Acceptance Criteria in seconds. PMs can edit any document inline, with every save automatically versioned. Prior versions are recoverable at any time via the History panel. Reference documents can be uploaded and used as AI context to keep generated content grounded in the product's reality.

### 3. Prioritise — Build Evidence-Based Roadmaps
Map insights to roadmap items with one click. Every roadmap item traces back to real customer evidence. PMs can always answer "why are we building this?" with data, not gut feel.

### 4. Execute — Streamline Sprint Planning
Convert roadmap items into sprint-ready user stories with pre-filled title, description, and acceptance criteria. Run multiple sprints, rename them, move tickets between sprints, and flag stories that need grooming — all before the planning meeting starts.

### 5. Communicate — Instant Sprint Briefs
Generate a structured stakeholder summary from actual sprint data in one click. Scope, user stories, readiness check, and success criteria — streamed live in seconds.

### 6. Learn — Build Institutional Memory
Store PRDs, design docs, and research in the Knowledge Base. The AI draws on this context when generating insights, briefs, and stories — so outputs are grounded in your product's reality, not generic templates.

## What PMAI Is Not

- It is not a replacement for human judgment, customer empathy, or stakeholder relationships.
- It is not a project management tool (Jira, Linear) — it feeds into them.
- It is not a generic chatbot — it is purpose-built for the PM workflow.
- It does not make roadmap decisions — it makes those decisions easier to justify.

---

## Design Principles

1. **PM-first** — every feature must save a PM meaningful time or improve decision quality.
2. **Evidence-linked** — every insight or suggestion must be traceable back to a source.
3. **Draft, don't dictate** — AI outputs are starting points; the PM always owns the decision.
4. **Context-aware** — the assistant understands the product, team, and history it operates in.
5. **Simple by default** — powerful features should not require a learning curve to access.
6. **Connected** — every step feeds the next; nothing is a dead end.

---

## Timeframe

- **Phase 1 (current):** Feedback Hub (keyword-based theme detection, zero hallucination), Pipeline Approval with verbatim evidence, Documents (AI-generated PRDs/UserStories/AC, inline editing, automatic version history, AI assistant with reference upload), Insights, Roadmap, Sprint Planner (multi-sprint, grooming, status tracking), Sprint Brief (AI-generated from real ticket data), Knowledge Base.
- **Phase 2:** Integrations (Jira, Linear, Notion, Zendesk), advanced feedback connectors, export to slides/PDF.
- **Phase 3:** Cross-team knowledge sharing, PM onboarding assistant, portfolio-level insights for Product Leaders.
